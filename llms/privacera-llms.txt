# Privacera (Trust3 AI)

> Privacera, now branded Trust3 AI, is a unified data and AI governance platform founded by the creators of Apache Ranger. It provides sensitive-data discovery, centralized access control and policy enforcement, encryption and masking, and access auditing across cloud data platforms (Snowflake, Databricks, AWS, Azure, GCP, Oracle), plus AI governance for LLM and agentic workflows, RAG/Vector DB access control, and prompt filtering. Its open-source project PAIG (Privacera AI Governance) delivers security, safety, and observability for GenAI applications.

## Products
- [Data Governance Agent](https://privacera.com/): sensitive data discovery, centralized access control & policies, encryption & masking, access audit & reporting, data stewardship, posture manager
- [AI Governance Agent](https://trust3.ai/): LLM and agentic-workflow governance, prompt filtering, Vector DB/RAG access control, policy enforcement and auditability
- [PAIG (open source)](https://paig.ai/): open-source guardrails for GenAI applications — security, safety, observability
